LD4 / LD2 rectangular reshape investigation

Does reshaping the two low-Q drain-load coils (LD4, Q≈4.4, 8.44 nH; LD2, Q≈1.2, 4.30 nH) from square to rectangular let them sit closer to the transistors they load, the way it does for LEQ? Answer, derived directly from klayout/make_lna_core_layouts.py and lna_core_layouts.json on main_pex: no — for both coils the limiting constraint is a fixed piece of other infrastructure, not the coil's own footprint, so no amount of reshaping moves the needle. No EM re-characterization was run; here is the algebra that makes the case, and what would actually have to change to get a real win.

01LD4: the 617 µm gap to cg

Chain: lna_core_input_match LEQ → keepout → lna_core_cg L1 → keepout → lna_core_cg LD4, all three on one X axis (make_lna_core_layouts.py, build_cg).

LEQ (input_match, Q≈12.4) L1 (= LSHUNT_OUT geometry reused, Q≈12.3) LD4 (Q≈4.4, the coil in question)

The formula (from build_cg, verified against lna_core_layouts.json)

leq_east = LEQ_PORT_X + LEQ.pe + LEQ.r = 36.0 + 157.875 + 158.375 = 352.25 cx1 (L1) = leq_east + SP_KO + L1.r = 352.25 + 25 + 211.295 = 588.545 ← matches JSON exactly cx4 (LD4) = cx1 + L1.r + SP_KO + LD4.r = 588.545 + 211.295 + 25 + 166.0 = 990.84 ← matches JSON exactly LD4 west edge = cx4 - LD4.r = cx1 + L1.r + SP_KO + LD4.r - LD4.r = cx1 + L1.r + SP_KO ← LD4.r cancels out completely = 588.545 + 211.295 + 25 = 824.84 gap (cg guard-ring edge x=208.3 → LD4 west edge) = 824.84 - 208.3 = 616.54 µm ≈ "~617 µm"

LD4's own half-size (geo["r"]) appears in the formula for cx4 (its centre) but cancels exactly when you compute its west edge — the only coordinate that matters for distance-to-cg. A rectangular LD4 with an arbitrarily small X half-extent would still have its west edge at x = 824.84, because that edge is defined as "L1's east edge + one keep-out", not "LD4's west edge". Every micron of reshape benefit the earlier LEQ work demonstrated came from LEQ's own footprint shrinking in the binding direction; LD4 is not in that situation — it is chained after a different, much bigger coil (L1, D≈423 µm vs LD4's own 332 µm), and that coil's bulk is what's actually 617 µm away from cg.

There is no Y headroom problem for LD4 at all — unlike the LEQ case, nothing north or south of LD4 blocks a taller coil. The only tight constraint is on the east side (4.16 µm to the VDD lead), which is irrelevant to cg-proximity.

Secondary possibility: shrink the macro, not the gap

Because LD4 east edge = LD4 west edge + 2×LD4.r, trading LD4's X half-extent for Y half-extent (same winding technique, same target L=8.44 nH, elongated vertically) would shrink MACRO_W by roughly twice whatever is shaved off LD4.r — e.g. shrinking LD4 from 332 µm to 232 µm across (-100 µm in X, using the 227+ µm of free Y headroom already confirmed above) could shrink the 1204 µm macro width by up to ≈100 µm once VDD4_X and the east ring are shifted in. That is a real die-area lever — just not the one asked about (it doesn't reduce the cg→LD4 trace length/parasitic at all), and the other three blocks (cs, output_match) don't need that width independently (checked: their own east edges have 100–280 µm of slack against MACRO_W), so the saving would actually be realized rather than immediately eaten by another block. Not pursued here since it doesn't address the stated concern, but flagged below as a possible separate follow-up.

Checked and ruled out: decoupling LD4 from the L1 chain

The task description raised the option of placing LD4 independently of L1 rather than chaining east of it — e.g. north of L1, directly east of cg's own C3 caps (which end at x=321.98, y ≤ 352.98), since L1 itself only reaches y=200.5 at its north edge, leaving the band y>200.5 geometrically "free" of L1. Checked against the other two blocks that share this same coordinate system (lna_core_cs and lna_core_output_match, confirmed via make_lna_core_parts.py: BLOCKS all four cells are drawn in one shared frame): that band is not actually free — lna_core_cs's own guard ring sits at x=279.7–374.54, y=389.7–426.97, and lna_core_output_match's LSHUNT_OUT coil spans x=454.5–877.1, y=390.7–813.3 — both squarely inside the candidate region. Decoupling LD4 from L1 is therefore not a cheap win sitting next to the reshape question; it would require re-floorplanning at least three of the four sub-blocks together, which is out of scope for a coil-reshape task. Flagged as a separate investigation below.

02LD2: the 64.5 µm gap to cs

LD2 sits north of lna_core_cs, ports facing east to the vx column (build_cs). The gap in question is purely in Y (their X ranges already overlap).

The constraints (from build_cs)

cx = LD2_PORT_X - LD2.pe = 340.0 - 108.0 = 232.0 cy = LD2_CY = 600.0 (fixed) assert cx - LD2.r - SP_KO ≥ max(vbias xn) + 1 → 232 - 108.5 - 25 = 98.5 ≥ 91 (slack 7.5 µm, X, vs vbias_cas column) assert cy - LD2.r - SP_KO ≥ max(vbias yj) + 1 → 600 - 108.5 - 25 = 466.5 ≥ 460 (slack 6.5 µm, Y, vs vbias jog line y=459) LD2 bottom edge = cy - LD2.r = 600 - 108.5 = 491.5 cs guard-ring top = 426.97 gap = 491.5 - 426.97 = 64.53 µm ≈ "~64.5 µm"

Why reshaping can't close this gap

The binding constraint on LD2's south edge is not the cs guard ring (426.97) — it's the vbias_cas jog routing at y=459, which needs a 25 µm keep-out from any coil. For a coil of half-size r, the smallest legal centre is cy_min(r) = 485 + r, which makes the achievable gap:

gap_min(r) = (cy_min(r) - r) - 426.97 = (485 + r - r) - 426.97 = 485 - 426.97 = 58.03 µm ← independent of r !

The floor is 58.03 µm no matter what shape or size LD2 is — reshaping, resizing, even shrinking LD2 to a point, cannot get it closer than that, because the limit is the vbias routing corridor, not LD2's footprint. Today's actual gap (64.53 µm) is only 6.5 µm above that floor, and that 6.5 µm is recoverable today by simply recentring the existing square coil (moving LD2_CY from 600 to 593.5) — no reshape, no EM re-run, no new GDS needed to capture the entire available gain.

Combined with the 6.5 µm proximity ceiling above, a rectangular LD2 was judged not to carry a benefit worth a new EM characterization campaign (methodology below would need ≈40+ minutes of openEMS per candidate geometry, likely several iterations to hit 4.30 nH with a new aspect ratio).

03Methodology note — why no EM run was launched

Per the task, the existing coil-characterization flow was reviewed before considering any new simulation: /foss/designs/rapidpassives/try4/newflow/ (build_real_coil.py, real_multilayer.py, scale_dout.py — the 5-metal-shunt, via-stitched spiral generator that produced LD4_coil.gds/LD2_coil.gds, driven through openEMS via run_*.py scripts against OPENEMS-GF180MCUD-1P5M-TM11KA-MIMB-STACKED4.xml). The most recent addition there, Ind2_LEQ_MAIN_D418.0_*.gds / run_leq_main.py (committed to main as LEQ_MAIN_coil.gds, commit 5e88190), was checked directly: its raw GDS bounding box is 450×450 µm — still square, just a bigger diameter for main's differently-sized input match. No rectangular-coil generator or rectangular coil GDS was found anywhere in the repo (any branch), the two other currently-locked agent worktrees (presumably the "related effort" mentioned in the task), or rapidpassives/. The claimed rectangular-LEQ precedent does not appear to have landed yet, so this investigation worked from the floorplan code directly rather than mirroring an existing rectangular pipeline. A single openEMS run in this flow takes ≈40 minutes wall-clock (see run_leq_main.log: 2461 s FDTD + setup); building a new independent-X/Y-scaling variant of build_real_coil.py and iterating it to a target L is a multi-hour effort that the quantified, near-zero proximity benefit above does not justify for either coil.

04Bottom line

  • LD4: do not reshape. The 616.5 µm gap to cg is set entirely by LEQ+L1's own bulk under the current east-of-L1 chaining; LD4's X half-extent cancels out of the governing formula. Y headroom is abundant (227+ µm south, 520+ µm north) so there's no "doesn't fit" problem to solve by reshaping either — LD4 already fits fine, it's just far away for reasons that have nothing to do with its own shape.
  • LD2: do not reshape. The 64.5 µm gap to cs floors at 58.0 µm regardless of shape (vbias-jog keepout dominates); only 6.5 µm is reshape/resize-recoverable, and that much is already recoverable today by recentring the existing square coil, with no new coil needed.
  • If floorplan area reduction (not proximity) is ever wanted: trading LD4's X for Y (same L=8.44 nH) could shrink the macro width by roughly 2× the X reduction — a legitimate, separate optimization, not evaluated further here since it doesn't address the stated cg-distance concern.
  • If cg→LD4 proximity is still wanted: the lever is decoupling LD4 from the L1 chain (placing it elsewhere), not reshaping it — and that was checked to not be a free win either (the obvious nearby space is occupied by lna_core_cs's guard ring and lna_core_output_match's LSHUNT_OUT coil), so it would need a dedicated multi-block floorplan study.
