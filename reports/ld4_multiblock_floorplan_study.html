01Why a grid search, not just eyeballing the floorplan

The prior report's spot-check of the "obviously free" band (y>200.5, north of L1; x>~322, east of cg's C3 caps) already found two occupying objects from other blocks in it: lna_core_cs's guard ring and lna_core_output_match's LSHUNT_OUT coil. But that was one candidate pocket, checked by hand. Since all four lna_core_* cells are drawn in one shared coordinate frame and assembled with zero relative offset (make_lna_core_parts.py: each block is top.insert(pya.CellInstArray(cell.cell_index(), pya.Trans())) — identity transform), the right way to answer "is there anywhere else" is to pull every drawn shape of all four blocks into one KLayout Region and test candidate LD4 boxes against it directly, rather than trust a by-hand reading of the floorplan sketch.

Method

for each of the 4 lna_core_*.gds (as currently generated on main_pex): region = merge(all shapes on all layers, excluding label layers [datatype 10]) total = union of the 4 block regions # single shared-frame obstacle map for candidate size (W, H): for candidate centre (x, y) on a 5-10 um grid inside the macro (0..1204, -253..851, inset by RING=12): box = W x H axis-aligned rectangle at (x, y) legal <-> box does not touch `total` (optionally enlarged by a clearance buffer) record the legal position with the smallest (west_edge_of_box - 208.3) # = the "gap to cg" metric used before

This automatically captures every obstacle from every block — guard rings, coils, MIM caps, resistors, bias/supply leads — with no risk of missing one the way a hand-sketch reading can.

05Bottom line

  • No code change was made. klayout/make_lna_core_layouts.py, the four lna_core_*.gds, and lna_core_layouts.json are untouched — there is nothing legal to switch LD4's placement to that would improve on today's 616.5 µm gap.
  • The macro is not under-packed near cg. The band that looks free in a floorplan sketch (y>200.5, x>~322) is in fact divided among six separate obstacles from three other blocks (L1, cg's own C2/R4, cs's guard ring, LD2, LSHUNT_OUT) with gaps of 13.7–80 µm between them — an order of magnitude short of the 332 µm LD4 needs.
  • This isn't a near-miss. An exhaustive position scan of LD4's exact footprint found zero legal spots anywhere else in the macro, even allowing bare (0 µm clearance) contact with neighbouring geometry. Sweeping every same-area rectangular aspect ratio from 40×2756 to 620×178 µm found legal fits only in a narrow band around 250×441 µm, and the one place those fit is farther from cg (683.7 µm) than today, past LSHUNT_OUT's east edge.
  • Getting LD4 meaningfully closer to cg is not a placement-tuning problem. It would require shrinking or relocating at least one of L1, the cs guard ring, LD2, or LSHUNT_OUT first to open a slot — i.e. re-floorplanning multiple blocks together, each with its own port routing and keep-outs to re-derive, which is out of scope for this study (and was already flagged as such in the prior reshape investigation).
