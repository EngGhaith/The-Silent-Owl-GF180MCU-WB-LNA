dev_ghaith_lowtailnoise_loraeu433

Attempted LoRa EU433 (433.92 MHz) relocation of the lowtailnoise variant; confirmed structural failure, gain never clears zero under the caps/R/bias-only constraint.

Structural failure

Design

Same RF-choked/bypassed tail as dev_ghaith_lowtailnoise. An extensive RLD3/CTUNE_M2 sweep was run trying to pull the CG-stage tank resonance down to 433 MHz without touching inductors, but gain hovers right at the zero crossing across the whole 433-440MHz range for every combination tried.

  • Swept RLD3 down to 1 Ω and CTUNE_M2 up to 20 pF
  • Gain hovers near the zero-gain crossing across 433–440 MHz for any RLD3/CTUNE_M2 combination tried
  • Best representative point left in the schematic: RLD3=1.5 Ω, CTUNE_M2=16 pF
  • Commit explicitly documents this as a confirmed structural failure, consistent with dev_ghaith_passive_output and dev_ilir_inductive_degen on this same band
