dev_ilir_loraeu868

dev_ilir retuned to LoRa EU868 (868.30 MHz); root-caused a series AC-coupling-cap voltage divider that the shunt-cap grid search alone couldn't fix.

Marginal

Design

Three-stage LNA core: a common-gate (CG) input stage (M2, actively source-degenerated by current-sink M5) feeds a cascoded common-source (CS) gain stage (M1/M4), with a source-follower buffer (M3) and a separate CS noise-cancellation branch (M9) summed at the output. This is the original IlirShala1 dev_ilir topology (PR #15), later rebiased by EngGhaith (PR #17, VBIAS_CG 2.2V -> 1.0V) to fix the CG stage's operating point. This branch relocates the passband to LoRa EU868, with the key fix being the series input-coupling cap C15 rather than the shunt tank caps.

  • Initial shunt-cap-only retune (b903d16) plus CTUNE_DEG fix (174357b) and joint-trim CTUNE_M2=30u, CTUNE_OUT=2u (7a67f64) left the amplifier non-functional.
  • Root cause: C15 is the series AC-coupling cap from rf_in_pad into M2's source; at 868.30MHz its reactance (122Ω) was 5x M2's own 1/gm (~26Ω after the 1.0V rebias), dropping most of the RF signal across itself.
  • Scaling C15 from 1.5p to 20p (8279f6c) restores its 2.4GHz-era reactance and crosses the design from non-functional to a working, if weak, amplifier.

History
