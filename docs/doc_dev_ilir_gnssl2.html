dev_ilir_gnssl2

dev_ilir retuned to GNSS L2 (1227.60 MHz), extending the shunt-cap retune with a CTUNE_DEG fix and a series-cap bump at C15.

Functional

Design

Three-stage LNA core: a common-gate (CG) input stage (M2, actively source-degenerated by current-sink M5) feeds a cascoded common-source (CS) gain stage (M1/M4), with a source-follower buffer (M3) and a separate CS noise-cancellation branch (M9) summed at the output. This is the original IlirShala1 dev_ilir topology (PR #15), later rebiased by EngGhaith (PR #17, VBIAS_CG 2.2V -> 1.0V) to fix the CG stage's operating point. This branch relocates the passband to GNSS L2, iterating through a degeneration-node shunt cap and a final series-cap correction.

  • Adds CTUNE_DEG shunt cap at M2's source-degeneration node (f56de9d placeholder, corrected in 6a4b6e8 after discovering it was tied to a stray net instead of M2's real source).
  • Joint-trims CTUNE_DEG=40u, CTUNE_M2=25u, CTUNE_OUT=10u (42004f2).
  • Extends retuning to the series coupling cap C15 (1.5p -> 3p) to recover gain lost to its growing series reactance at the lower frequency (1ed7de6): S21 improved from +5.25dB to +8.37dB for essentially unchanged match quality.
